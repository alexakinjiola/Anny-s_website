How to Order
from Annyshub

No complicated checkouts. No failed payments. Just browse, pick what you love, and send us a WhatsApp message. We handle the rest.

4 Simple Steps to Your Perfect Piece

1

Browse the Collection

Take your time exploring the website. Use the category filters or browse by price range and material. Every product page shows real photos, verified buyer reviews, full pricing including delivery, and all available sizes and colours.

2

Choose Your Piece

When you find a piece you love, note the full product name, your preferred material (gold plated, rose gold, silver), your size or chain length, and your delivery address. All of this is shown clearly on the product page so there is no guessing.

3

Send Your WhatsApp Order

Tap the Order via WhatsApp button on any product page. A pre-filled message will open in WhatsApp with the product details already included. Just add your name, delivery address, and any specific requests — and hit send. We respond within minutes during business hours.

4

Pay & Receive

Once we confirm your order, we will send you our bank account details for transfer. Payment is confirmed instantly and we begin packaging your order right away. Lagos orders typically arrive same day or next day. Other states within 2–5 working days.
